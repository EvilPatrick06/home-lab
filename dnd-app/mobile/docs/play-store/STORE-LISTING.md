## Full description

> Dungeon Table Online is a virtual tabletop (VTT) for running tabletop RPG
> sessions on your phone or tablet.
>
> • Character sheets — build and manage 5e characters with a guided builder and
>   level-up flow.
> • Maps & tokens — a full tactical map with fog of war, dynamic lighting, area
>   templates, and pinch-to-zoom touch controls.
> • Dice — animated 3D dice with a reduced-motion option.
> • Multiplayer — host or join a table; play with friends peer-to-peer using an
>   invite code.
> • Library — browse monsters, spells, items, and rules.
> • Optional AI Dungeon Master — bring your own model/API key.
>
> Dungeon Table Online is a fan-made tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed
> by Wizards of the Coast. Game rules content is used under the applicable open
> license (see the in-app About screen).
