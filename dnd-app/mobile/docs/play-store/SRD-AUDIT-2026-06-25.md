# SRD / IP Content Audit — bundled rules data (pre-publish gate)

_Audit date: 2026-06-28. Scope: all rules/content data bundled in the mobile
(Expo) app via `mobile/assets/embed/data` — identical to the source tree at
`src/renderer/public/data` (both contain the same 3,041 JSON/asset files).
Method: programmatic scan of the `source.book` tag carried by every content
entry, plus filename/term scans for Product Identity (PI) names, cross-checked
against the official SRD content list._

## HEADLINE: BLOCKING — non-SRD copyrighted content is bundled

**There IS non-SRD, copyrighted Wizards of the Coast content bundled in the app,
including full stat blocks for Product-Identity creatures. The app must NOT be
published in its current state.** This is a copyright blocker independent of
attribution: CC-BY attribution only licenses content that is actually *in* the
SRD. It does not license (a) text taken from the full copyrighted rulebooks
beyond the SRD, or (b) Product Identity (e.g. Beholder, Mind Flayer) — those are
excluded from every SRD and cannot be relicensed by adding an attribution line.

## Evidence 1 — every entry is tagged to a copyrighted core book, none to the SRD

A scan of the `source.book` field across all parsed content entries returns the
following sources. **Zero entries are attributed to the SRD or CC-BY.** The
content is tagged as sourced from the full 2024/2025 D&D core rulebooks:

| Entries | `source.book` value (normalized variants grouped) |
|---|---|
| 723 + 33 + 8 + 2 | **2024 Dungeon Master's Guide** (`2024 Dungeon Masters Guide`, `DMG 2024`, `Dungeon Master's Guide`, `Dungeon Master's Guide (2024)`) — 248 of these are magic items |
| 434 + 133 + 29 + 9 | **2024 Player's Handbook** (`2024 Players Handbook`, `PHB 2024`, `2024 Player's Handbook`, `Player's Handbook (2024)`) |
| 300 + 12 | **2024/2025 Monster Manual** (`2025 Monster Manual`, `2024 Monster Manual`) |
| 1 | `2024 Core Rules` |
| 1 | `Homebrew` (a `_template.json`) |
| ~14 | misc inline rule citations (spell/feature names used as a "source" string) |

The official SRD released under CC-BY-4.0 is **SRD 5.1** (2014 rules) and
**SRD 5.2 / 5.2.1** (2024 rules). Content lawful to redistribute would be tagged
to, and limited to, those documents. Here it is tagged to the **full books**
(PHB / DMG / Monster Manual), which are copyrighted and far larger than the SRD
subset. Tagging alone does not prove infringement of every line, but it shows the
dataset was built from the books, not the SRD, and (next section) it demonstrably
includes material the SRD excludes.

## Evidence 2 — Product-Identity creatures shipped as full stat blocks

`dm/npcs/monsters/**` contains **563 monster stat-block JSON files.** The SRD's
monster list is a deliberately limited subset and **explicitly excludes Product
Identity creatures.** The bundle ships full stat blocks for PI creatures that are
NOT in any SRD, including:

- **Aberrations:** `beholder.json`, `mind-flayer.json` (+ `mind-flayers/` colony,
  arcanist), `aboleth.json`, `kuo-toa*.json` (x4), `slaad*.json` /
  `*-slaad.json` (x6: tadpole, blue, red, green, gray, death),
  `githyanki-*.json` (x4), `githzerai-*.json` (x4)
- **Monstrosities:** `displacer-beast.json`, `carrion-crawler.json`,
  `umber-hulk.json`, `yuan-ti*.json` (x7 incl. malisons, abomination,
  infiltrator)
- **Constructs:** `modron-*.json` (x5: mono/duo/tri/quadro/pentadrone)
- **Undead:** `beholder-zombie.json`

> Note: a few PI names (Beholder, Mind Flayer, Umber Hulk) are *mentioned in
> passing* in SRD 5.1 prose, but their **stat blocks remain Product Identity and
> are not under CC-BY** (confirmed against community/legal summaries of the SRD
> CC release). Shipping their stat blocks is the infringing act, regardless of
> the incidental name mentions.

## Evidence 3 — trademarked settings / named IP referenced in content

Term scan across the data (filenames + content) found references to trademarked
WotC settings and named IP, e.g.: **Forgotten Realms** (19 files), **Waterdeep**
(10), **Baldur('s Gate)** (6), **Neverwinter** (4), **Faerun** (1). These are
Product Identity setting marks, not open content. (Some other flagged terms —
*Tiefling, Drow, Tarrasque* — DO appear in the SRD and are fine; they are listed
here only so the reviewer can distinguish them from the genuine problems.)

## What this means for publishing

1. **Do not build or upload the current bundle.** Shipping it to Google Play
   would distribute infringing content (PI stat blocks + full-book-sourced text)
   and also risks Play's IP-impersonation policy.
2. **Attribution / extra docs do not fix this.** Adding an `ATTRIBUTION.md` or an
   About-screen credit is necessary for the genuinely-SRD content, but it cannot
   license the PI creatures or the non-SRD book text. The blocker is *what is
   bundled*, not *whether it is credited*.
3. **Free-vs-paid does not fix this.** The app being free reduces damages
   exposure but does not make redistribution of copyrighted/PI content lawful,
   and Play's IP policy applies to free apps too.

## Remediation options (decision for the maintainer — this audit does not delete anything)

- **Option A — SRD-only rebuild (recommended for a clean publish).** Replace the
  dataset with content drawn *only* from SRD 5.1 and/or SRD 5.2.1, re-tag every
  entry's `source.book` to the SRD, and **remove all PI creature stat blocks
  listed above** plus the trademarked-setting references. Then re-run this audit
  to confirm `source.book` shows only SRD/CC-BY values and the PI term scan is
  clean.
- **Option B — license the content.** Obtain a license from the rights holder for
  the non-SRD material. (Not available via the open license; a business decision.)
- **Option C — do not publish to a public store.** Keep the full dataset for
  private/personal use only (no redistribution), and publish only an SRD-safe
  build to Play.

## Reproduce this audit

```bash
cd dnd-app/src/renderer/public/data/5e   # (== mobile/assets/embed/data/5e)
# 1) source.book breakdown (should be SRD-only after remediation):
python3 - <<'PY'
import os,json,collections
c=collections.Counter()
for r,_,fs in os.walk("."):
    for f in fs:
        if not f.endswith(".json"): continue
        try: d=json.load(open(os.path.join(r,f),encoding="utf-8"))
        except: continue
        s=d.get("source") if isinstance(d,dict) else None
        if isinstance(s,dict) and s.get("book"): c[s["book"]]+=1
        elif isinstance(s,str): c[s]+=1
for b,n in c.most_common(): print(f"{n:5d}  {b!r}")
PY
# 2) PI creature stat blocks (should be empty after remediation):
find dm/npcs/monsters \( -iname '*beholder*' -o -iname '*mind*flayer*' \
  -o -iname '*illithid*' -o -iname '*githyanki*' -o -iname '*githzerai*' \
  -o -iname '*slaad*' -o -iname '*umber*hulk*' -o -iname '*displacer*' \
  -o -iname '*kuo*toa*' -o -iname '*carrion*crawler*' -o -iname '*yuan*ti*' \
  -o -iname '*aboleth*' -o -iname '*modron*' \)
```
